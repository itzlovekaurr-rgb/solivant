SOLVANTA RENEWABLES — Website Demo
===================================
Open index.html in a modern browser. The site is a standalone front-end demo.
Features: responsive layout, dark/light theme toggle, mobile menu, solution tabs,
solar flow component interactions, monthly/annual electricity-unit estimator,
gallery filters/lightbox, FAQ accordion, form validation demo, and reduced-motion support.

The photos are loaded from Unsplash URLs and therefore require an internet connection.
The consultation form does not transmit or store data. Contact details are placeholders.
Before publication, connect a real form backend, verify contact details, review privacy/legal
copy, and check image licensing/availability.
